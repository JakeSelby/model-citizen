# Decisions and plans

- **Pointing at an option is not approval** — put it in the review artifact; build only on an
  explicit "build" or "go with N".
- **Once the user has approved a multi-step plan, do not re-ask at each step** — execute, log, move
  on — while no new information materially changes it and the action was in the approved plan.
- **Re-surface for confirmation** when an error or unexpected state makes a step unsafe, a step was
  not in the plan, or the blast radius grew. Shapes: `plan-authoring`; asking, ceremony: the stances.
