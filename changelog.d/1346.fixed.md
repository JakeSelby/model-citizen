The `context-reset-handoff` adherence answer now records whether the context actually came back
down after the hand-off block, apart from whether the handoff was saved. A save used to be the
whole answer, written at once, so a session that saved and then ran on far past the threshold
read as `followed`. The response now carries `context_reset` (`compacted`, `cleared`,
`not_reset` or `unknown`) and waits for it, or for a day; a `/clear` is noted from its
`SessionEnd` reason.
