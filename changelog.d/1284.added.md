The Touch ID or login-password dialog that guards a paid Studio run, an apply, a rollback or a recovery now comes from Model Citizen, with its own icon, and its reason line says who is asking, what will happen and why: "Model Citizen wants to ..., so ...", or "Model Citizen Studio" when the request came through the Studio. A small helper app is compiled and ad-hoc signed on your Mac from source in the repository the first time it is needed, and its signature hash is checked before each use; without the developer tools the check asks through `osascript` as before. `scripts/presence_check.py` now says which program asked.
