Studio now shows rule health under Reports, reached from the Library: one row per loaded rule
with the status and reason `citizen usage --rules` prints, each detector's hits over 7, 30 and 90
days, when it last fired, its labelled-corpus precision, its soft context-cost estimate and its
ablation effect. Every figure is the owning engine's own, hit counts are labelled exploratory,
a rule whose detector is under the precision floor or has no measured precision has its hits
marked unreliable, and a column with no evidence reads "not measured" with the reason. "Try
without it" creates a new draft with the rule switched off and its draft test ready to run;
nothing live changes.
