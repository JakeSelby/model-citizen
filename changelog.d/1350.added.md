A `decisions` stance chooses how a question reaches you. `numbered-block`, the default, is unchanged: numbered blocks in chat, answered by typing, and carries the "no chooser widget" line out of the always-loaded `decisions-and-plans` rule. `decision-page` puts every decision on one private page per project, with the recommendation marked, a control that accepts every recommendation and a free-text answer that overrides the options; answers are applied only when you say so, and the page never collects a secret. The new `decision-page` skill carries the page contract, the item schema, the apply step and a generic template. Under `decision-page`, a plan's Review Card keeps its decisions numbered and the message posting it links the page. Runtimes with no artifact tool fall back to numbered blocks and say so.
