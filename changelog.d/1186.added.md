`scripts/layer_scorecard.py` builds one deterministic per-layer scorecard from registered production, rule-task, long-session, sweep and judge rows: prefix tokens and USD per run by model, behaviour against bare and against the layer's own removal, the outcome and cost effects and the keep, trim or no-evidence verdict, under a harness-against-bare headline per stratum with equivalence verdicts, refusing exploratory rows unless `--allow-exploratory` marks the whole report exploratory, and an evidence bundle may carry it.
