The `balanced` and `frugal` cost stances, the cache-hygiene rule and the docs now describe sessions
that reset their own context: a hand-off at 90% of the reset point (360,000 and 180,000 tokens),
then an automatic compaction at 400,000 or 200,000 that is reseeded from the saved handoff, with no
new session. Their `compaction` switch is now `auto-at-reset`, and the `cache-hygiene/compact`
detector no longer counts that automatic compaction, while a manual `/compact` still counts.
