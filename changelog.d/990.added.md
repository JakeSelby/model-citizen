Studio now compares two finished replay targets side by side, paired by task. The candidate's
harness rows are compared with the base's through `replay_stats.compare`, and every reading,
interval and trial count is shown as the engine reports it, so an interval that spans no effect
reads inconclusive whatever its point estimate. Two targets that ran different task sets, models,
trials, evaluator packs or per-trial budget caps, or that did not finish what they requested,
are refused with each difference named; a draft side shows when it has
gone stale, and `citizen runs compare` gives the same answer from the command line.
