## Evidence and interface obligations for future delivery

- Studio consumes the landed engine's versioned results, including two-arm, four-cell and variable-arm designs. Unsupported formats and unknown/failed inputs remain visible.
- Sampling and power controls distinguish exploratory work from proof. Matched draft comparisons retain task/model/trial identities and become stale after relevant edits.
- Paired intervals, ratio nulls, zero-pass/undefined results and proof status agree exactly with CLI analysis; no independent browser estimator is introduced.
- Target/config identities, raw artifacts, spend/cancellation accounting and rebuildable indexing survive integration of shared-runner changes.
- Later field/Codex reports retain runtime and assignment provenance and do not pool incompatible runs; proposal generation never automatically changes stored policy.

These are scheduled acceptance obligations, not claims of implementation or validation. The UI branch's richer pending design artifacts remain governed by #961/#962; this amendment does not transplant or overwrite that branch's design work.
