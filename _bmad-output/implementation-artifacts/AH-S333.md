## Story

As a harness user, I want every reply judged on its way out against the rules a pattern can't check, so that a broken rule is caught during the session, not found in the transcripts afterwards.

## Context and value

Hooks enforce only what a pattern can decide, and most rules need judgment; today those are measured after the session ends, if at all. #801 calibrates a compliance judge and #140 binds Jev to the shared runtime events, but nothing puts a judge on every turn. #135's re-scope dropped prose checks because nothing consumed or scored them; here the turn consumes the ruling and #377's labels score it.

Depends on #140, #377 and #801. #755 prices the calls.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** a turn ending on Claude Code or Codex, **when** Jev judges it, **then** each judged rule's ruling is recorded in the ledger with the rule, the verdict, its confidence, cost and latency.
2. **Given** a pack below its pre-registered κ floor (#801), **when** it rules, **then** its rulings stay in shadow and change nothing.
3. **Given** the provider is unreachable or past its timeout, **when** the turn ends, **then** it ends as it would without Jev.
4. **Given** live rulings for a rule, **when** adherence is reported, **then** the live rate appears beside ruleprobe's after-the-fact rate.
