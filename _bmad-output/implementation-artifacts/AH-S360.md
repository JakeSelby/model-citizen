## Story

As a developer, I want the Studio to read as a clean, white, data-first operational tool, so that the numbers and their status carry each page and nothing else catches the eye.

## Context and value

The Studio follows the "Briefing" direction with the "Clear" palette (DESIGN.md, 2026-09-23): a headline summary, a two-by-two grid of report cards and generous padding. In use it reads card-heavy, with too much margin. On 2026-10-06 the operational direction, option A of five rendered studies, replaced it. Only the look changes: routes, data and workflows stay as they are.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The shell is a Mantine AppShell: a 220 px white navbar with a hairline border, a 52 px header holding the page title, the version and context, and the page's CLI command, and a white canvas.
2. The Hub has no cards: one number strip split by dividers, then sections separated by single hairlines, with compact tables (rows about 30 px, 13 px text, monospace only for ids, commands and numbers that need to align).
3. One muted teal accent; red, amber and green carry status only.
4. Configure, Library, Experiments, Activity, Reports and Setup use the same density, with tables and hairline sections in place of cards and papers.
5. A matching dark theme and the one-column phone layout.
6. The #999 accessibility suite stays green: contrast, focus, 320 and 390 px reflow and the planted defects.
7. `studio/dist` is rebuilt, the bundle manifest verifies, and the #1000 budgets hold: 360 KiB script and 48 KiB style gzipped, 1500 KiB raw.
8. DESIGN.md records the direction as an amendment, not a rewrite, and `studio/src/theme.ts` and `studio/src/styles.css` carry it.
9. Before and after screenshots of every page are captured with the end-to-end tooling.

## Design

### Approach

Theme and layout only. Replace the "Clear" palette with one muted teal accent (the target's active navigation item is `#087f5b` on `#e6fcf5`), hairlines `#e9ecef`, text `#212529` and secondary text `#868e96`; status red `#c92a2a`, amber `#e67700` and green `#2b8a3e`. Move the shell to Mantine `AppShell`. Replace cards and papers with the number strip, hairline-separated sections and compact tables on every page, and derive the dark theme from the same tokens.

### Decisions and alternatives

- Chosen 2026-10-06: option A of five rendered studies replaces the Briefing direction and the Clear palette.
- The target names Inter and JetBrains Mono, loaded from a web font service. The Studio makes no network requests, so name both first in their font stacks with system fallbacks, and bundle no font file: bundling one needs a licensing review.
- Status colours keep AA contrast on both canvases: adjust the shade, never drop the status.

### Do not implement

- Route, data or workflow changes.
- New product copy.
