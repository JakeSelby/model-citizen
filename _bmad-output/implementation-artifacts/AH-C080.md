## Goal

The lint measured the always-loaded set (the global instructions, every rule and the longest
variant of every stance) at about 4,364 of 4,822 tokens and 223 of 225 lines, so the next line that
loads always would fail the cap. This chore gives back room under the existing caps with
meaning-preserving cuts only, and leaves the plain-language rewrite to #813.

Decided by the owner on 2026-09-26: trim rather than raise the cap, to at least 10 lines of
headroom, cutting only text stated twice, pure restatement, or detail the file already points to a
skill or document for; no operative instruction is dropped, softened, reworded or moved out of the
always-loaded set.

**Target:** at most 213 of 225 lines (at least 12 lines of headroom, 10 of them restored) with the
token figure below its cap. **Result:** 213 lines and ~4,204 tokens, from 223 lines and ~4,364
tokens.

## Dev notes

How the count works: `always_loaded_groups` in `bin/harness` sums raw lines, blank lines and
headings included, and characters over `CHARS_PER_TOKEN`. A stance counts only at its longest
variant, so a cut in a stance whose variants tie for longest gains nothing; the voice stance's
`concise.md` (33 lines against 9 for the next) held the most room. Cuts were chosen where removing
the text lets the edited paragraph re-wrap one line shorter.

Cuts (file; removed text; where its meaning still lives), ten lines in all:

1. `primitives/stances/voice/concise.md`, first paragraph (1 line): "pick the shape by what the reply
   is for," restates the attributed "let the shape of the answer match the shape of the problem",
   which stays word for word because `third-party.json` records it.
2. `concise.md`, shapes paragraph (1 line): ", so the reader can answer by number" is the reason for
   numbered options; "numbered options, one line each" stays.
3. `concise.md`, general paragraph (2 lines): "Whatever the shape, the first sentence is the result,
   the answer, or your question," summarises the opener the shapes paragraph already gives each
   shape (done message one line, answer first sentence, report outcome first, decision opens with
   the question, brief bottom line first, deep dive summary first, draft is the draft); and the
   label "Skip the ritual:", whose list stays whole.
4. `primitives/rules/secrets.md` (1 line): "rule files, docs, source, config," are instances of "any
   file that is committed to source control"; the entries that are not obviously files (comments,
   docstrings, commit messages, story files, planning artifacts) stay.
5. `secrets.md` (1 line): the two-line credentials bullet becomes one line, "Read credentials from
   the environment, a cloud CLI profile (`--profile`) or a secret store; never inline, never a
   pasted key.", which keeps every allowed source and both bans; the explanatory "Cloud CLIs
   resolve them" wording stays in `docs/how-it-works.md` (secrets section).
6. `primitives/rules/voice-and-format.md` (1 line): "on Claude Code the `brief-guard` hook appends
   one when a brief states no return bound" describes a hook, not an instruction; it lives in the
   hook, `docs/runtime-controls.md` and `plan-authoring`.
7. `primitives/rules/decisions-and-plans.md` (1 line): the decision block's shape, "each question
   stated unambiguously, the assessment, a recommendation with reasoning, the alternatives with
   their honest case", lives in `plan-authoring` "Presenting decisions", which the rule points to
   as "Shapes: `plan-authoring`".
8. `decisions-and-plans.md` (1 line): "choosers suit trivial forks whose labels carry full meaning"
   is the converse of "No chooser widget for substantive decisions"; its test for a trivial fork
   lives in `plan-authoring` "Presenting decisions".
9. `primitives/instructions.md`, projected to `claude/CLAUDE.md` (1 line): "Always-loaded context
   is capped and `citizen lint` enforces it, so" is the maintainer's reason, in `harness-authoring`
   "The always-loaded cap"; "installed at user level" restates the heading "Global instructions".

Considered and not taken: the delegation stance's `citizen role run` sentence (the spawn hook's
refusal quotes it and the native evidence records it), the concise voice's example pair
(attributed and wanted by #813), and cuts in stances whose variants tie for longest.
