## Story

As a harness maintainer, I want a cheap replay tier that reports whether one intended mechanism
fired, so that I can smoke-test a behavioural change the day it is made and keep the production
set for a calibration run per release.

## Context and value

Every question of the form "does delegation fire on a many-file read" or "does the stop gate fire
on a red tree" costs a production-model run today. A pinned small model on three small
oracle-scored tasks answers it for cents. The answer must stay visibly weaker than production
evidence: it says a mechanism can fire in this fixture on this model, not that the harness saves
cost or how the production model behaves.

The maintainer amended the issue's cost criterion before the build: the micro campaign is five
pre-registered reps under a 4.55 USD stop, and a two-rep run cannot be cited. Since the first
attempt at this story, the replay refuses every same-repository task before any model call
(#1077), which includes these three; the tier is built and verified, and its paid run waits on
that control as well as on spend approval.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the micro manifest, **when** `replay --tier micro --verify-tasks` runs, **then** every
   parent fails its held-back oracle, every solved fixture passes it and the parent gate is green,
   with no model call.
2. **Given** a complete micro set, **when** its rows are scored, **then** each reports pass or
   fail, the mechanism id, fired yes, no or unknown, and cost, read from stream counters or named
   offline detectors; unreadable evidence is unknown, never no.
3. **Given** no overrides, **when** the tier is selected, **then** it pins
   `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`, five reps, a 0.10 USD run cap, 0.05 USD preflights and a 4.55 USD
   stop, and refuses another model, a pair and a live run without `--raw`.
4. **Given** micro results, **when** history is written, **then** their tier, series and files are
   separate from production, a history file refuses the other tier's row, and no ratio or verdict
   is emitted.
5. **Given** the change, **when** the gate runs under `python3` and `/usr/bin/python3`, **then**
   lint is clean and the suite is green. The campaign's reported cost and its pace under 15 minutes
   are observed by the pre-registered paid run, a follow-up.

## Design

### Approach

Extend the container replay rather than add a runner. `--tier micro` selects the manifest, pinned
model and caps through `resolve_tier`; the arms, preflight, contamination control, raw streams and
held-back scorer stay authoritative. After offline detection, `replay_micro.score_rows` adds each
row's mechanism result, the results file is rewritten whole, one line per run is printed, and a
complete, pre-registered set writes the micro history.

### Decisions and alternatives

- Five reps and a 4.55 USD stop, the maintainer's amendment; thirty runs and two preflights report
  3.10 USD at their caps, and the stop covers the soft cap's overshoot. Two reps was rejected: a
  two-rep run cannot be cited.
- The manifest pins the model and `--model` cannot override it, so a series cannot change model
  silently. Flags may still lower caps or change reps.
- The production series keeps its seed; the micro series adds `|micro`. The first attempt reseeded
  both, which would have rotated the production series with no change to its tasks.
- `--pair` is refused: a pair's arms answer a different question from whether a mechanism fires.
- The contamination control is kept for this tier. Exempting it is a call on evidence policy,
  left to the maintainer.
- No ratio, verdict or SM-2 block in the micro history; reusing the production verdict would
  overclaim from a smaller model and a mechanism fixture.

### Do not implement

No paid run and no pre-registration: spend is not approved. No exemption from, or change to, the
contamination control. No production-model claim, savings claim or `product.json` line.

## Dev notes

### Bound decisions and requirements

- Both arms run the same fresh-container protocol and differ only by the harness.
- Every cost or efficacy test is pre-registered; an exploratory run writes no history row.
- Unknown evidence stays unknown: a detector miss is `false` only when its stream was read.
- The replay refuses a task whose answer is readable in the harness arm's installed checkout.

### Files to touch

- `benchmarks/micro/tasks.json` — the tier manifest and pinned model.
- `benchmarks/oracles/micro_*.py` — three held-back checks with their solvers.
- `scripts/replay_micro.py` — validation, scoring, report and micro history.
- `scripts/cost_bench.py` — tier resolution, preflight cap, history routing and guard.
- `tests/test_replay_micro.py` — offline regression coverage.
- `docs/benchmarks.md`, `changelog.d/512.added.md` — the operator contract and release note.

### Testing

`tests/test_replay_micro.py` covers manifest validity and each malformed mechanism, the pinned
values at the parent commit, tri-state scoring, the report line, pinned defaults and the cap
arithmetic, the refusals, the preflight cap, the kept contamination refusal, a complete set
writing only the micro history, the separate series and the exploratory case.
`python3 scripts/cost_bench.py replay --tier micro --verify-tasks` printed `verified 3 task(s)
... 0 error(s)`.

## Review findings

- First-attempt audit: a manifest naming another tier entered the micro path, and duplicate or
  non-string detector ids crashed or stayed unknown. Both are refused and tested.
- Rebuild: the first attempt reseeded the production series; the seed is restored.
- Open: the contamination control refuses the three tasks, so the paid run needs tasks of
  independent provenance or a maintainer decision on this tier.
