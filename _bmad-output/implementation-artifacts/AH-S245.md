## Story

As a harness maintainer, I want Jev's compliance packs calibrated on hand labels, with agreement and bias audits, so that the compliance judge of record is shown to agree with people before its verdicts gate anything.

## Context and value

FR-49 asks for evidence gates at each stage. Jev's own evaluation reports calibration error with bootstrap intervals, but on a small synthetic set, and a compliance judge of record needs calibration against human labels. #377 hand-labels seed fixtures; this calibrates the compliance packs on them and reports how far the judge agrees with people.

Depends on #377 and #140. Traces to FR-49 and PRD §9 (v0.16.0).

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given #377's labels, then each pack reports Cohen's κ against them, a confusion matrix, and expected calibration error with bootstrap intervals.
2. Given bias audits for position, verbosity and same-family preference, then each pack reports them.
3. Given a pack below its pre-registered κ floor, then it is not used as a judge of record.
4. The PRD records κ and the bias audits through `bmad-prd`, since FR-49 names calibration error only.
