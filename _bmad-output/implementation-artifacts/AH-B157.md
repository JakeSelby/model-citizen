## Reproduction

The registered micro campaign (#1131) was launched at `--run-cap 0.10 --spend-cap 4.55` on `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` at effort high. The bare arm's preflight finished two turns at 0.0188 USD. The harness arm's preflight stream ended with `result` subtype `error_max_budget_usd`, `total_cost_usd` 0.0556 and `num_turns` 1, before its first tool result came back, against the 0.05 USD preflight cap. The replay printed `the harness arm's gate is red in its own container: no reply` and refused with exit 2. No scored trial ran.

## Root cause

Two defects. The micro caps in `scripts/replay_micro.py` were set below the harness arm's fixed first-turn cost: that turn writes a cold 27,350-token instruction prefix to the 1-hour cache at 2.00 USD per million, 0.0547 USD before any output, against a bare first turn of 0.0152 USD (13,541 of its 20,467 prefix tokens were read warm). So the preflight cap could not be met, and the 0.10 USD run cap left the harness arm about 0.04 USD for a task against the bare arm's 0.08. Separately, `preflight` in `scripts/cost_bench.py` judged only the gate output, and the refusal printed the empty `result` text as "no reply" without reading the stream's `subtype`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the micro tier, **when** its constants are read, **then** the preflight cap is at least twice the measured harness first turn, a run leaves the harness arm at least 75% of its cap after that turn, the ceiling for 3 tasks x 5 reps x 2 arms plus two preflights is at most 8.00 USD, and the stop is at most the ceiling; the measured costs and headroom sit beside the constants.
2. **Given** a preflight stream ending `error_max_budget_usd`, **when** the replay refuses, **then** it prints a budget stop naming the arm, the reported cost and the cap, and never "no reply".
3. **Given** the #1131 pre-registration, **when** read, **then** its deviation log carries a dated entry with the new caps, ceiling, stop and amended command, appended without editing the registered text.
4. **Given** docs/benchmarks.md, **when** read, **then** the micro protocol states the new figures.

## Design

Caps from the saved preflight streams at `policy/prices.json` list prices: harness first turn 0.0556 USD cold, bare 0.0152 (0.0409 if fully cold), a warm turn about 0.003 for either arm. Run cap 0.25 USD (4.5x the harness first turn, leaving it 0.19 USD), preflight cap 0.15 USD (2.7x, room for a Stop-hook re-entry), ceiling 30 x 0.25 + 2 x 0.15 = 7.80 USD, stop 7.80 USD. `preflight` records `budget_stop` and `cap_usd` on each check from the parsed `subtype`; the refusal prints the budget stop instead of the red-gate line. A budget stop does not change whether a preflight passes: that is still the gate's own output. Rejected: a stop above the ceiling to absorb soft-cap overshoot, which the 8.00 USD budget does not allow; a run cap of 0.15 USD, which still leaves the harness arm a third less than the bare arm. The production tier's caps do not change: on claude-sonnet-5 the same turn costs 0.111 USD against a 0.25 USD preflight cap.

## Regression test

`tests/test_preflight_budget_stop.py` replays a synthetic `error_max_budget_usd` stream (`tests/fixtures/preflight/budget-stop-harness.jsonl`): the refusal test fails before the fix, which printed "no reply", and passes after it; a third test prices the fixture's first turn from `policy/prices.json` and holds the micro caps to AC1.
