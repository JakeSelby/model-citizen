## Story

As a harness maintainer, I want a proof bundle, a `harness evidence verify` command that re-derives it, and evidence cards that tie each published claim to its rows, so that no claim, "cheaper" included, is published without evidence that supports it.

## Context and value

PRD §9 draws the MVP line: v0.15.0 ships when `harness evidence verify` passes on proof set 1, whatever it shows. FR-54 keeps "cheaper" out of the landing copy until a result supports SM-2's hypothesis. This builds the bundle, the verifier, and the evidence cards that tie a published claim to the rows behind it.

Depends on #794 and #795. Traces to FR-54, NFR-5, NFR-13 and PRD §9 (the MVP line).

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given a proof bundle, when `harness evidence verify` runs, then it re-derives every published figure from the bundle's rows and fails on any mismatch.
2. Given the evidence standard (#794), then the verifier checks its twelve items and fails when the pre-registration postdates the first trial.
3. Given a published claim, then it has an evidence card: the claim, its estimand label, figure, interval, bundle and verify status.
4. Given `product.json`, then the landing copy cannot state a measured claim unless its card's bundle verifies, and a test proves the refusal.
5. Given proof set 1, then it is published with its "what we do not claim" section, whatever it shows.
6. The PRD records evidence cards through `bmad-prd`, since the text of record does not name them.

## Design

### Approach

Use a versioned directory bundle with a strict JSON index and relative, hashed artifact references.
The index names trial JSONL, the frozen task manifest, plan and GitHub merge receipt, price table,
arm records, full redacted trajectories, audit records, published figures and evidence cards.
Verification is read-only and offline: never execute a command supplied by a bundle. Recompute
costs from per-model token counts, headline statistics through `replay_stats`, per-task summaries,
ICC/design effects, fallback counts and schedule completion. Compare every declared figure and
card with those derived values. Retain failures and unpriced trials; unavailable values are null.

Check all twelve evidence-standard items explicitly. A missing, malformed, contradictory or
inapplicable-without-reason item fails with an item-numbered diagnostic. Validate relative paths
and resolved containment before reading artifacts. Check plan content and task-manifest bytes
against the recorded local Git revision, plan ancestry and committer time against the earliest
trial, and the captured GitHub merge receipt's repository, PR, revision and merge timestamp.
The receipt is retained provenance, not a cryptographic attestation by GitHub.

Bind product claims to cards that name their exact product field and text. Re-run the card's bundle
verification in the landing-copy check, without trusting a saved success flag. Recognizable cost,
speed and efficacy claims without a card fail closed; neutral capability descriptions remain valid.
The supported SM-2 decision and interval bound determine whether cheaper or a magnitude is allowed.

### Decisions and alternatives

- Use the standard library and existing statistics rather than a new analysis dependency.
- Keep structural audit evidence distinct from mechanically re-derived figures: the verifier cannot
  prove arbitrary natural-language assertions or recover observations that were never recorded.
- Permit the evidence standard's explicit not-applicable cases only; replay sample ratio is required.
- Keep proof-set publication pending until fresh registered trials exist; synthetic fixtures exercise
  the verifier and never stand in for proof set 1.

### Do not implement

Do not perform paid model calls from the verifier, execute bundled commands, publish a savings
claim from fixtures or host-profile logs, replace the approved statistical decision rule, or silently
exclude failed, timed-out or fallback attempts.

unknown prices, model fallback, figure/interval mismatch, unsupported claims and false cached
verification status. Add an actual CLI subprocess test; no live services or model calls.
