## Story

As a developer relying on the harness's configurability claim, I want the boundary between invariants, stances, settings and presets published and every switch tested end to end, so that the claim matches what the harness actually enforces.

## Context and value

The claim that every opinion is a switch is stronger than the implementation, and independent policy surfaces can contradict selections ([#130](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/130)). This story is the epic's acceptance boundary: it depends on #118 to #129, and a 2026-09-18 comment keeps it open until upstream dependencies land, checks are refreshed against `main`, and the compatibility release gate has native evidence ([#130 comment](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/130)).

The epic's 2026-09-21 assessment rated it Partial: ownership docs and stance validation exist, but there is no invariant boundary doc, no effective-policy diagnostic, no presets and no interacting-pair tests ([#116 comment](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/116)).

Since that assessment, PR #473 (for #464) shipped four constraints in `primitives/constraints.json`, so some invalid combinations are now refused, and `harness stances --json` reports them [Source: primitives/constraints.json]. `harness stances` prints each dimension as `name=variant` followed by the variant's behaviour text; `--json` adds `source`, which is the variant file's path rather than the layer it came from, alongside the fixed precedence list, adapter coverage and conflicts [Source: bin/harness#cmd_stances]. FR-2 is itself partial: sync ignores `HARNESS_STANCE_*` variables that `harness stances` honours (#294). No document under `docs/` states an invariant boundary, and named modes are planned rather than shipped (FR-16, #557).

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** each change issue under #116, **when** its record is inspected, **then** it names an owner surface, a default, its migration behaviour and its test evidence, and every documented variant resolves and renders.
2. **Given** every individual variant and the interacting pairs cost/context, voice/style, review/delegation, testing/verification/gates and autonomy/external-actions, **when** the suite runs, **then** each is tested.
3. **Given** an old configuration, **when** sync and uninstall run, **then** it stays valid, user-owned values are preserved, an invalid combination fails before any mutation, and unsupported native enforcement stays labelled.
4. **Given** a candidate for merge, **when** it is checked, **then** the exact CI gates run on the committed head under the Python floor and a current Python, with isolated sync, projection checks and native probes where supported, and native qualification and deployment stay separate.

## Design

### Approach

The issue proposes publishing the invariant, stance, setting and preset boundary and an ownership matrix across rules, skills, workflows, settings, hooks and detectors; adding layered tests and effective-policy diagnostics; offering presets plus optional advanced customization instead of asking every new question during init; and replacing the absolute claim with "strong defaults, explicit trade-offs and configurable workflow preferences" ([#130](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/130)).

The plan names the four classes: invariants (truthful results, uncertainty, secret protection, authorization and native restrictions), stances, settings, and presets that never override an explicit user setting [Source: docs/plans/stance-completeness.md#Step 2 — Selection contract]. It requires coverage of every variant plus the named pairs, without claiming an exhaustive Cartesian product [Source: docs/plans/stance-completeness.md#Step 6 — Verification and migration].

### Decisions and alternatives

- Pairs, not the full product of variants, are tested [Source: docs/plans/stance-completeness.md#Step 6 — Verification and migration].
- Presets and an optional advanced path were chosen over asking every question at init ([#130](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/130)).
- The always-loaded context cap is kept, not raised to fit more switches [Source: docs/plans/stance-completeness.md#Step 6 — Verification and migration].

### Do not implement

- Native qualification and deployment are separate from this story ([#130](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/130)).
- Do not sync an unmerged candidate into the live installation.
