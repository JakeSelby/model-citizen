## Story

As a solo or small-team agentic developer, I want the old pages and redirects gone and the redesign shipped, so that the Studio has one IA and the landing copy says so.

## Context and value

Plan step 8. Once every page has landed, the redirects from S1 and the old pages are removed, `product.json` and the Studio docs updated, and the full gate plus the Studio workflows run.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** every new page merged, **when** this story lands, **then** the old pages and the redirect table are removed.
2. **Given** the redesign, **when** `product.json` and the Studio docs are read, **then** they describe the new IA.
3. **Given** the branch, **when** the full gate and the Studio workflows run, **then** they pass.
4. **Given** the removal, **when** tests run, **then** no test references a removed route.

## Design

### Approach

- Delete old pages and redirects; update `product.json` and docs.

### Decisions and alternatives

- **Remove redirects at ship:** they served only the transition. Rejected: keeping them indefinitely.

### Do not implement

- New features.

## Tasks

- [ ] Remove pages and redirects (AC: 1, 4)
- [ ] `product.json` and docs (AC: 2)
- [ ] Full gate and Studio workflows (AC: 3)
