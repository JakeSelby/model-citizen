## Story

As a developer, I want evidence-backed stance proposals I can try in a draft, so that the harness suggests improvements while I stay in charge.

## Context and value

Stance drift proposals from the decision log (#692) arrive in an inbox with their evidence. "Try it" opens a draft with the proposal applied and queues its test; the developer applies it, or dismisses it with a reason. Evidence proposes and the developer applies (PRD §8).

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given a proposal, then the decision rows and counts behind it show with it.
2. Given "Try it", then a draft opens with the proposal applied, and nothing live changes.
3. Given a dismissal, then its reason is recorded, and the same proposal is not raised again without new evidence.
