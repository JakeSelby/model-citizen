## Story

As a developer who runs several agents, I want write topology and delegation depth to be selectable separately from the model-tier stance, so that I can allow isolated parallel writers without weakening role confinement.

## Context and value

The issue says fixed rules prohibit parallel writers and all redelegation regardless of isolation, and that model selection, permission to delegate, write topology and review independence are four separate decisions ([#120](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/120)). The epic's 2026-09-21 assessment rated it Not delivered: `delegation/` carries launch and model variants only, and "writes stay single-threaded" is fixed ([#116 comment](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/116)).

On `main` today the `delegation` stance still has only `off`, `session-model` and `tiered` [Source: primitives/stances/delegation/], and `primitives/rules/delegation.md` still says "Writes stay single-threaded (tool list, not prompt); no subagent re-delegates its assignment." The issue's problem statement is partly out of date: the builder role now expects a sibling builder in another worktree of the same repository, and carries `delegation: none` [Source: primitives/roles/builder.md]. [ASSUMPTION: the rule now reads as single-threaded per agent rather than a ban on parallel builders in separate worktrees; no document states that reading.]

The issue carries the v0.15.0 milestone.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** parallel writers, **when** they are launched, **then** each has its own worktree and declared ownership, and parallel writes into one shared checkout are rejected.
2. **Given** a delegation depth bound, **when** a child requests a nested spawn, **then** the depth holds, `off` still prevents unsolicited delegation, and a child's authority never exceeds its parent's or its role's.
3. **Given** the isolated role-worker design, **when** the topology is integrated with it, **then** conflict detection, missing isolation, nested requests and native capability gaps are each tested.

## Design

### Approach

The `delegation` stance keeps launch and model preference. Two settings are added: `delegation_writes` with `serial` or `isolated-worktrees`, and `max_delegation_depth`, default 1, meaning the root may spawn and children may not. Constrained roles keep `delegation: none`. An unsupported topology is rejected instead of silently relaxing authority ([#120](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/120)).

The plan adds that constrained workers remain unable to redelegate, and that any change to the worker contract needs its own confinement evidence [Source: docs/plans/stance-completeness.md#Step 3 — Existing opinions].

### Decisions and alternatives

- `serial` is the default write topology [Source: docs/plans/stance-completeness.md#Step 3 — Existing opinions].
- Rejecting an unsupported topology was chosen over silently relaxing it ([#120](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/120)).

### Do not implement

- Do not let constrained roles delegate.
- Do not widen a child's authority beyond its parent or its role.
- Do not sync an unmerged candidate into the live installation.
