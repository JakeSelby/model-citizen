## Story

As a developer, I want an opt-in, AI-generated overview of recent system health and performance on the Hub, so that issues and anomalies surface with an advisory list of what to investigate.

## Context and value

The Studio UX spec plans this overview in its "AI Health Overview" section: opt-in, advisory, never the system of record, with every claim linked to its source evidence. It needs pluggable providers and anomaly detection over the evidence the Studio already keeps. No story covers it yet.

## Acceptance criteria

1. No model call happens until you enable the overview and accept its cost policy, and every attempt reserves its worst-case spend against a daily cap before it starts.
2. The overview reads immutable evidence only (system-health, hook-performance, comparison, rule-health and usage snapshots) and links each claim to its source snapshot and field.
3. Providers are pluggable through a replaceable adapter to your configured model or tool; the overview records the provider, model, tool and template versions it used, and stores no provider credentials.
4. Generation runs read-only, in a scrubbed environment with no write route, shell or action tool.
5. It is advisory: its investigation rows are labelled as generated suggestions, distinct from alerts, and it cannot create, clear or grade an alert, apply a proposal or change a stance.
6. The Hub shows the overview's freshness, coverage, spend and cap; a stale, refused or failed refresh keeps the last good overview marked stale, with the reason.
7. Tests cover each criterion.
