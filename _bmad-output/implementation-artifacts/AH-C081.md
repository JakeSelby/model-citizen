## Design

- **Scope recorded first.** The PRD gains §4.15 Unattended maintenance with FR-71 to FR-74 and a
  v0.15.0 roadmap line, through `bmad-prd`'s update intent with a memlog entry. `epics.md` gains AH-E021,
  its nine story lines and a coverage row, through `bmad-create-epics-and-stories`.

  fix) were not answered at approval, so each affected story records the plan's recommendation as its
  default pending confirmation and lists the alternative: AH-S279, AH-S278, AH-S280.
- **Nothing that runs.** The bot's workflows, App, secrets, labels and code are the children's work.
