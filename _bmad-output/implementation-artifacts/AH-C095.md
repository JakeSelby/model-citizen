## Goal

Allow validated planning-only changes to land when CodeRabbit deliberately excludes their files,
without weakening review requirements for code, agent instructions or review-policy changes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given a complete planning-only diff, when its allowed paths, file modes and content are checked,
   then CodeRabbit may be recorded as not applicable with the exact base/head and validation evidence.
2. Given a mixed diff, rename crossing the boundary, executable file or policy change, when review
   eligibility is assessed, then normal CodeRabbit review remains required.
3. Given either route, when merging, then the existing Gate, required CI, ownership and unresolved
   thread conditions still apply.

## Design

Keep the repository-specific rule in `.claude/rules/working-here.md` and replace the unconditional
AGENTS.md sentence with a pointer to that authority. The allowlist permits Markdown planning records
and the two exact generated tracking files; content and file-mode checks prevent a path-only bypass.
Do not change CodeRabbit filters or branch protection. This policy PR uses normal review.
