## Story

As a harness maintainer, I want a runner that screens switch permutations with fractional factorial designs and then tests the survivors in combination, so that many modules can be screened in far fewer runs than a full factorial needs.

## Context and value

PRD §1's fourth comparison screens permutations of switches, within and across layers, with fractional factorial designs, then tests the survivors in combination. Two-arm and two-by-two designs test a few modules at a time; screening many needs a design that estimates main effects in far fewer runs than a full factorial.

Depends on #790, #795 and #796. Traces to PRD §1 (comparison 4) and §9 (v0.17.0).

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given k switchable modules, when the runner builds a design, then it states its resolution, and main effects are not aliased with two-way interactions.
2. Given a finished screen, then each module's main effect on pass rate and Cost-of-Pass is reported with an interval and labelled screening.
3. Given a module the screen flags, then no claim is made until a two-arm run confirms it.
4. The PRD gains the requirement through `bmad-prd`, since comparison 4 has no FR.
