## Story

As a developer, I want every rule's measurement status, adherence and cost in one table, so that I can prune dead rules and fix weak ones.

## Context and value

One table of every rule: measured, dark or unmeasured with the reason (#647), how often it is followed and its trend, its detector's precision, its context cost (#514), and when it last fired. A rule that costs tokens and never fires can be switched off in a new draft from its row, and the developer decides whether to apply it; nothing is switched off for them.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given `citizen usage --rules`, then the table shows the same status and reason for every rule.
2. Given a dark rule, then "Try without it" opens a draft with the rule switched off and its test ready to run.
3. Given a detector under the precision floor, then its adherence figure is marked unreliable.
