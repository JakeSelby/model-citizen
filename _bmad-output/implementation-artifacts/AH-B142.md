# AH-B142 — grade-bash timing tests fail on wall-clock under parallel suites

## Reproduction

Run three full suites in parallel (load average 5 to 6). The ratio check in
`test_grading_a_hundred_kilobyte_command_stays_well_inside_the_hook_timeout` failed at 9.67 and
8.34 against its limit of 8; both passed on an immediate re-run with the grader unchanged.

## Root cause

Each grading takes tens of microseconds, because both command sizes pass the scan cap. The test
timed single gradings with `time.perf_counter`, so one preemption or timer tick in the quarter-size
sample moves the full-to-quarter ratio past 8. The grader's growth did not change; the machine's did.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** three CPU-bound processes running, **when** the grade-bash timing tests run,
   **then** they pass.
2. **Given** a grader with an injected quadratic loop, **when** the tests run, **then** the
   ratio check fails, and a quadratic loop large enough to pass a tenth of the hook timeout
   fails the budget check.
3. **Given** the existing limits (a tenth of the tightest PreToolUse timeout, a ratio of 8),
   **then** they are unchanged.

## Design

A shared `cpu_seconds(call)` helper in `tests/test_grade_bash.py` measures `time.process_time`
over batches of about 20 ms and keeps the best of five, so waiting for a core is not charged and
microsecond calls are not lost in the clock's resolution. The grade-bash scaling tests in
`tests/test_grade_bash_quoting.py` and the capped-scan speed check in `tests/test_neutralize.py`
guard hook speed the same way and move to CPU time too.

Raising the limits was rejected: it weakens the quadratic guard and still couples the result to
load. Wall-clock checks that guard a hang, a lock wait or a deadline, not CPU work, stay as they
are: `test_usage.py` (malformed stdin exits quickly), `test_credential_probe.py`,
`test_usage_feed.py`, `test_usage_feed_race.py`, `test_usage_feed_settling.py`,
`test_adherence_settle.py`, `test_usage_export.py`, `test_intents.py` and
`test_workspace_session_hook.py`. No production code changes.

## Regression test

The changed tests are the regression tests. In a scratch copy, a quadratic `find` loop in
`_grade_text` failed the ratio at about 13, a heavier one at about 16, one past the budget at
1.27 s against 1.0 s, and a quadratic loop in `_sql` failed the scaling test at 11. Under three
CPU burners the three timing tests passed 30 of 30 runs.
