# AH-B173 — fix(cost): the fresh-session thresholds are ignored on 1M-token windows

<!-- bmad-sync:begin -->

This work item was authored as part of the repository's committed BMad planning system.
<!-- bmad-sync:end -->

## Reproduction

1. Run a Claude Code session under the `balanced` cost variant on a 1M-token window until its
   context passes 160,000 tokens.
2. The feed says the fresh-session line once at 120,000 and once at 160,000; nothing else happens,
   and the session continues.
3. An exploratory audit of 265 host sessions found 57% passed 160,000, ran a median of six more
   turns (105 calls) after it, and 53% of all spend landed after that point. Per main-thread call:
   $0.13 at 50-160k, $0.17 at 160-200k, $0.23 at 200-400k, $0.43 above 400k.

## Root cause

- `session_nudge_at` was chosen against a 200,000-token window, where the client's own limit ended
  the session soon after the line; on 1M windows nothing does [Source:
  primitives/stances/cost/balanced.json].
- The line is soft by design and is the only signal: no turn-boundary step asks for the handoff
  [Source: policy/hooks/usage-feed.py#session_line].
- The line names a size, not what continuing costs, so it gives no reason to act.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** a shipped cost variant, **when** it is resolved, **then** it names a soft threshold
   (`session_nudge_at`), a hard one (`session_handoff_at`) and a `context_cost_curve`: `balanced`
   160,000 and 400,000, `frugal` 160,000 and 200,000, `max` none; `off` blocks nothing.
2. **Given** a context past the soft threshold, **when** the next prompt is fed, **then** the line
   names the threshold and the cost multiple from the curve.
3. **Given** a context past the hard threshold in an interactive session, **when** the turn ends,
   **then** the Stop is blocked once with a reason asking for the handoff and a fresh session.
4. **Given** that block, **when** the next Stop arrives, **then** it is released whatever the turn
   did; a context that falls back under the threshold re-arms it.
5. **Given** a block, **when** it is emitted, **then** a `fresh-session-handoff` adherence row is
   written, answered `followed` when the session ends within two prompts.
6. **Given** a headless run (`CLAUDE_CODE_ENTRYPOINT` `sdk-*`), **when** it stops past the hard
   threshold, **then** nothing blocks unless `HARNESS_HANDOFF_BLOCK=on`.
7. **Given** a red stop gate, **when** the turn ends, **then** the gate's block answers alone and
   the hand-off is not spent.

## Design

- Two new sidecar switches, validated in `posture.py`: `session_handoff_at` (whole positive size
  or null) and `context_cost_curve` (ascending `[size, multiple]` pairs). The soft threshold is
  the existing `session_nudge_at`, so no variant authored elsewhere breaks.
- The soft threshold sits where the cost curve leaves its floor, 160,000, in every variant that
  nudges: under it a call costs the same however full the context is. Variants differ only in the
  hard threshold: `balanced` 400,000 (3.3x the floor, 25% of sessions), `frugal` 200,000 (1.7x).
- `usage-feed.py` answers `Stop`: it advances the transcript reader under its lock, compares the
  context with the hard threshold, and keeps the threshold blocked at in its state, which makes the
  block one-shot. The lifecycle asks the stop gate first and the feed only when the gate released.
- Rejected: a Stop block on every turn past the threshold (a trap); blocking at the soft threshold
  (half of all sessions, at 1.3x); a pricing lookup in the hook (the curve is posture data, and the
  hook holds no number of its own).
- Must not change: the soft line's once-per-threshold rule, the feed's silence on any failure, and
  the stop gate's own block and release rules.

## Regression test

one-shot block, its release and re-arm, headless skipping and its switch, the adherence row and
its reading, sidecar validation, and the lifecycle's Stop composition.
