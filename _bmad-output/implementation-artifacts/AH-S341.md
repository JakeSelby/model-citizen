## Story

As the maintainer, I want a removal arm for every layer that costs tokens or turns, judged by a
pre-registered rule, so that each layer is kept or trimmed on evidence rather than by assumption.

## Context and value

The #514 and #1148 sweep covered rules and stances only. The hooks, the skill, agent and command
listings and CLAUDE.md also cost tokens or turns, and had no arm and no rule saying what result
would justify them. Evaluator pack v1.3.0 now holds a rule-targeted task per layer and the
long-session scenarios, and #1181's equivalence module gives every score a bounded margin. The
scorecard (#1186) needs one verdict per layer.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** `benchmarks/ablations.json`, **when** it is loaded, **then** it holds an arm for each rule, each stance with an `off` variant, the hooks that act in a headless session (the delegation nudge, test-output filter, usage feed, stop gate and grade-bash among them) and the skill, agent and command listings each as a whole, and names CLAUDE.md and the stances without an `off` variant as not buildable, with reasons.
2. **Given** each arm, **when** the sweep is planned, **then** it names its layer, what it removes, its own pack v1.3.0 `rules` tasks, its long-session scenarios where relevant, and how its removal is verified, and every arm also runs the ten-task outcome subset.
3. **Given** this commit's resolver, **when** the entries are checked, **then** every arm resolves, a listing that leaves a unit on or carries an unbound unit is refused, and a core hook's removal carries the acknowledgement the resolver requires.
4. **Given** a sweep's rows, **when** `justify` runs, **then** each layer reads keep, trim or no evidence by the pre-registered margins, with its marginal cost beside it.
5. **Given** the pack, **when** `scripts/ablations.py plan` runs, **then** it lists every arm with its tasks and prints a one-rep ceiling per model.

## Design

### Approach

The manifest stays schema 2 and gains optional keys: per arm `layer`, `what`, `tasks`, `long_session`
and `scores`, and manifest-wide `justification`, `pack`, `outcome`, `pricing`, `unbuilt` and
`excluded`. A listing's `removes` is a list keyed `kind/*`. `ablations.schedule` takes a per-task arm
filter, so an arm runs only the named tasks it maps. `sweep_plan`, `price_plan` and `justify` are
pure functions over the manifest and rows; `main` reads the pack by `git show`.

### Decisions and alternatives

- A listing carries the units the module manifest binds to it, because the resolver refuses the bare listing. A combined arm was rejected because it would attribute two listings' effect to one.
- CLAUDE.md, autonomy and plan-ceremony are listed `unbuilt`. An image-level omission, or swapping in another variant, would change what the arm measures.
- Six hooks that never fire in a headless replay are `excluded` with reasons, so the sweep does not pay for arms that toggle nothing.
- Cost-control layers carry a `Cost-of-Pass ratio` score, so a layer that saves cost without moving a behaviour score can still be kept by the rule.
- The price is a ceiling: `max_turns` at an assumed per-turn envelope, capped at the run cap. Scenarios use their own cost cap.

### Do not implement

No change to `summarise`, no long-session runner and no new stance variant. No image-level omission for CLAUDE.md.
